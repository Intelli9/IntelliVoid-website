Include your account email, a short description of the issue and any error message. Please do not send passwords, API keys or client databases. Redact personal information from screenshots.

Account and privacy requests

For access, correction or deletion enquiries, email connect@intellivoidai.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Within IntelliEstate, account deletion is available under More → Privacy and account → Delete my account. It requires your current password. Contact us for help with an individual contact record.

Website enquiries

This website does not contain an account login or an enquiry form. Contact links open your email application. If you email us, IntelliVoid Ltd uses the information you provide to respond and manage your enquiry through Google Workspace.

The website’s own code does not set analytics or advertising cookies. Vercel hosts the site and may process technical request information to deliver and protect it. This does not mean that hosting is free of logging.
