MEET INTELLIESTATE

Past enquiries.
Fresh perspective.

An app for agents who want to stay personal as their contact list grows. Start with the context you already have, then decide where a conversation belongs next.

01 / BRING THE CONTEXT

Start with your records.

Import a CSV of past contacts and notes. Review the import before background processing begins.

02 / FIND A REASON

Review the suggestion.

See why a contact was suggested alongside supporting excerpts. Historical notes are context, not proof of current buying intent.

03 / MAKE IT PERSONAL

Put your name to it.

Edit and save a draft, dismiss it, or open a reviewed message in your phone’s email composer. Nothing is sent automatically.
