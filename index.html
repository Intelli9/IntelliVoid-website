WHEN YOUR CONTACT LIST OUTGROWS YOUR TIME

The viewing ended.
The follow-up never happened.

Between viewings, calls and negotiations, it’s easy to leave old enquiries untouched. IntelliEstate helps you pick up those conversations without reading every old note and writing every message from a blank page.

01 / UPLOAD YOUR CONTACTS

Stop digging through spreadsheets.

Import a CSV of past contacts and notes. Review the import before background processing begins.

02 / REVIEW WHO TO CONTACT

See why a contact was suggested alongside supporting excerpts. Historical notes are context, not proof of current buying intent.
